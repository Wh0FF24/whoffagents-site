# Whoff Agents LLC

**AI-native engineering. Useful software, automation, and autonomous systems.**

We turn human intent into systems people can use, inspect, and improve. Our work connects research with practical engineering for businesses, product teams, researchers, and organizations with a concrete technical problem.

## Core capabilities

- **Software engineering:** Web applications, custom tools, and product development shaped around the people who use them.
- **AI agents and automation:** Agent workflows, tool integration, and explicit points for human oversight.
- **Systems integration:** APIs, connected workflows, and data exchange across tools and services.
- **Testing and evaluation:** Prototyping, failure analysis, and evidence-based iteration around the outcome that matters.

## How we work

Define a useful outcome. Build a focused implementation. Inspect the result and use the evidence to choose the next step. AI participates throughout research, development, testing, and review; people retain technical ownership, judgment, and final authority.

**Verification over trust.** Research progress, stronger capabilities, and a clearer understanding of a problem are worthwhile outcomes alongside delivered products.

## Current research

**Persona Fleet** is an in-development research program in cellular privacy and cyber deception. It investigates whether realistic decoy activity can make observable communications less informative to an adversary. The program challenges its approach with its own detectors. Protection effectiveness has not been established.
